## International Case Studies & Precision Motion References

* **Wafer Scanner Control Overview:** *Control of Wafer Scanners: Methods and Developments* — covers wafer scanner subsystem architecture, vibration isolation, and sub-nanometer positioning requirements across exposure cycles: [MIT Fab Lab PDF](https://fab.cba.mit.edu/classes/865.24/topics/mechanical_design/mechanical_design/precision-frontier/Control_of_Wafer_Scanners_Methods_and_Developments.pdf)
* **Multi-Stage Lithography Dynamics:** *On characterization of a generic lithography machine in a multi-body / stage framework* (Al-Rawashdeh, Al Janaideh, Heertjes, *Mechanism and Machine Theory*) — examines reticle/wafer stage dynamics, inertial disturbances, and fine positioning stages: [TU/e Research Repository PDF](https://research.tue.nl/files/196152956/1_s2.0_S0094114X21003712_main.pdf)
* **Coarse-Fine Step-Scan Positioning:** *Ultra-Precision Step Stage for Silicon Wafer Scanners* (Bowen Zeng, University of Michigan Engineering report) — analyzes coarse vs. fine stage partitioning, vibration/dwell trade-offs, and stage thermal effects: [University of Michigan Engineering PDF](https://scholarships.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/36/2018/06/2017-Wang-Chu-Chien-Wen-Report-Bowen-Zeng.pdf)
* **Sliding-Mode Precision Motion Control:** *Global Iterative Sliding Mode Control of an Industrial Biaxial Gantry* — illustrates sliding-mode compensation for nonlinear and uncertain tracking dynamics in precision motion axes: [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.12580v2)
